Data the app processes

NickClick stores the information you enter to organise your collections of toy figures:

  • App users: a name and, if you choose, an icon, an emoji or a profile photo. No email, phone number, date of birth or any other personal data is requested.
  • Collections: name, image and what you use them for (just collecting, or collecting and playing).
  • Figures: photos, name, name history, traits, notes, favourites, background colour and, if the collection allows it, family relationships between characters.
  • Missing list: names, notes and, if you add one, a reference photo, plus the collection's total if you state it.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app's information for advertising or analytics.
  • We do not request sensitive data for normal use of the app.

Storage and iCloud sync

Data is stored on your device and, if you have iCloud turned on, synced through CloudKit in the private database of your own iCloud account, so your collections are available on your iPhone and your iPad. That information is only accessible with your Apple account: GitoCorp Studios has no access to it and does not receive it on any server of its own. Syncing is governed by Apple's iCloud terms.

NickClick has no accounts of its own and uses no third-party servers. Each user created in the app sees only their own collections. If you have not signed in to iCloud, the data stays on the device only. To delete the synced data, delete the app's data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Photos and recognition

Background removal on photos and the “Who is it?” feature, which compares a photo with the figures in your collection, are performed with Apple's vision tools on the device itself. Photos are not sent to any external service to do this; they are only synced with your private iCloud as part of your collections.

Device permissions

  • Camera: only when you tap “Camera”, to photograph a figure, a collection or a profile photo.
  • Photos: the app uses the system photo picker, which only gives it access to the images you choose; it does not access the rest of your photo library.

NickClick does not request access to location, microphone, contacts or calendar, and does not send notifications.

Children

NickClick can be used as a family. The app does not collect personal information from children or from anyone: user names are only labels inside the device and do not leave it. We recommend that adults supervise younger children's use of the camera.
