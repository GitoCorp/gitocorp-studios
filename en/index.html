Home library

Lomos

Your home library on your iPhone: scan the barcode and Lomos fills in the title, author and cover; draw your bookcases room by room and find any book on its shelf.

  • ISBN scanning with data from Google Books, Open Library, Apple Books and the National Library of Spain; for brand-new books, a photo of the cover is enough.
  • A map of each room with your furniture drawn and your real books on their shelves.
  • Family readers with their progress, times read, ratings, loans and a wishlist.
  • A library shared with your family through iCloud.

Deduction game

Disfrazados

A deduction game for children aged 6 and up and the whole family: someone has taken the party cake and you must find out which costumed guest did it, using yes-or-no questions.

  • Different guests in every game, with questions asked by tapping pictures.
  • Against Mask, in a duel, in the Daily Challenge or in the “Your turn to answer” and “It's getting away!” modes.
