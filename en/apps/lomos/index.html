Your books, in their place.

Lomos redraws your bookcases — Billy, cube units, corner units, bedside tables or made to measure — with the spines of your real books, so the whole family knows where each one is.

Scan and done

Point at the barcode and Lomos brings the title, author, pages, synopsis and cover. Also by title, in batches or by hand with a photo of the cover.

The map of your home

Rooms and walls seen from the front, with every piece of furniture to scale, each spine in its colour and as thick as its page count. Tap a book and its shelf lights up.

Family readers

Each reader with their progress, readings and ratings. Loans to friends, public-library books with a return reminder and a wishlist with shops and prices.

Getting started

A quick guide to get your home library ready in an afternoon.

1. Create your library

The first time you open Lomos, give it a name and say who you are. Add the readers at home: you manage the little ones without a phone.

2. Add your books

Tap “+” and scan the barcode on the back cover. If it doesn't show up, search by title and author or add it by hand with a photo of the cover.

3. Draw your furniture

In “Map”, create rooms and walls and choose each piece's model: wide or narrow Billy, cube unit, corner unit, bedside table, chest of drawers, made to measure or on your own photo.

4. Place each book

Choose the shelf when adding a book or move several at once from the shelf. Shelves with decorations or games can be marked so they don't count.

5. Find and read

Type in “Where is it?” and Lomos takes you to the shelf. Note the page you're on and Lomos works out the percentage.

6. Share with your family

From “Family”, invite another adult with their own iPhone: you will see and edit the same library with your own iCloud accounts.

Lomos privacy

Books, readers, bookcases and loans are stored on your device and in your private iCloud, and are only shared with your family if you invite them. To fill in a book's details, the ISBN or title is sent to Google Books, Open Library and Apple Books. No accounts of our own, ads or analytics.
