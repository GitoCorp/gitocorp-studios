RealFood Planner is an app by GitoCorp Studios. The public brand is GitoCorp Studios and the responsible developer is Jorge Sanz Borrell. For privacy or support enquiries, you can write to gito@gitocorp.com.

Data the app processes

The app uses the information needed to plan meals and organise family use of the app. It may store dishes, weekly menus, pantry, shopping list, meal history, preferences and planning settings.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app's information for personalised advertising.
  • We do not request sensitive data for normal use of the app.
  • The information entered by the user is used to display and organise the app's own features.

Storage and sync

RealFood Planner can work with data stored on the device. In versions with family sync, the app may use iCloud and CloudKit to share dishes, weekly planning and related data between the devices or members invited by the user.

When iCloud is used, the information is stored in the user's Apple account and shared only with the people they invite. GitoCorp Studios does not maintain a server of its own for this data and does not access the content stored in the user's iCloud account.

Device permissions

The app will only request permissions when they are needed for a specific feature. For example, if a version adds photos of dishes or notifications, iOS will ask for authorisation before allowing access. The user can accept, refuse or change those permissions in system settings.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If any privacy-related issue is detected, it can be reported through the contact email.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app adds new features, external services or relevant changes in how it works. The version published on this page is the current reference.
