The highest card, with a twist.

Carta Alta turns the simplest game into a duel of decisions: when to flip, which powers to bring and when to use them.

Power cards

Steal, Twist, Shield, Double or nothing and more. Each player builds their power deck from those they've earned in the shop and chooses when to use them.

Fair and fast

A balanced deal, with the same cards for both players in a different order, and a machine that plays by the same rules without peeking at your cards.

Decks your way

Four illustrated themes, collectible card backs, a different table mat each game and decks with photos of your family as the figures.

Carta Alta privacy

Carta Alta stores on the device the player profiles, points, powers and photo decks. Photos are chosen one by one, cut out and have their background removed inside the iPhone, and never leave it. No accounts, ads, purchases or connection.
