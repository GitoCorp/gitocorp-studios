Noche de Juegos is an app by GitoCorp Studios. The public brand is GitoCorp Studios and the responsible developer is Jorge Sanz Borrell. For privacy or support enquiries, you can write to gito@gitocorp.com.

Data the app processes

The app uses the information needed to organise a personal or family game library. It may store games, plays, challenges, notes, ratings, preferences and data related to organising game sessions.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app's information for personalised advertising.
  • We do not request sensitive data for normal use of the app.
  • The data entered by the user is used to show their library, history and challenges inside the app itself.

BoardGameGeek and intermediary server

Some search or import features may query BoardGameGeek to obtain public information about board games, such as title, number of players, duration, description, year of publication or associated images.

When the user performs an online search or imports information from BoardGameGeek, the app sends the search text or the game's identifier to an intermediary server of GitoCorp Studios (hosted on Supabase), which forwards it to BoardGameGeek to obtain the results. Your plays, ratings and other library data are not sent. GitoCorp Studios does not control BoardGameGeek or its servers; requests made to that service may be subject to its own terms and policies.

Storage

Library, play and challenge data is stored on the device. In addition, the app uses iCloud (CloudKit) to store in your iCloud account the covers available offline, your reviews and your shared library, so they are available across your devices.

If you share your library with other people, that data will be visible to those you invite. Apple manages this data under its own privacy policy; GitoCorp Studios does not access it and the app has no user accounts of its own. If a future version adds another storage system or reviews shared between users, this policy will be updated beforehand to explain it clearly.

Device permissions

If the app allows adding photos from the photo library, it will ask for permission before accessing that feature. If notifications are turned on, iOS will ask for authorisation beforehand and they can be turned off in system settings.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If any privacy-related issue is detected, it can be reported through the contact email.
