Infiltrado is an app by GitoCorp Studios. The public brand is GitoCorp Studios and the responsible developer is Jorge Sanz Borrell. For privacy or support enquiries, you can write to gito@gitocorp.com.

Data the app processes

The app uses only the information needed to set up and play in-person games. Specifically, it stores on your device:

  • Player groups that you create (for example, “Family” or “Friends”), with their name and the names or nicknames of their members.
  • The last names used, to suggest them in the next game.
  • Words already played, so they are not repeated until the chosen categories are used up.

You type the names yourself and they can be nicknames; there is no need to use real names. Each game's configuration (mode, categories, timer) is only used while you play.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app's information for personalised advertising.
  • We do not request sensitive data for normal use of the app.
  • The app works without a user account.
  • It contains no advertising or analytics or tracking tools.

Storage

All of Infiltrado's information is stored only on your device. The app does not use iCloud, has no user accounts, does not need an internet connection and does not send your data to any server, whether GitoCorp Studios's or a third party's. You can delete your groups from the app at any time; if you delete the app, all its data is deleted with it.

If a future version adds sync, Game Center, achievements, leaderboards or other online features, this policy will be updated beforehand to explain it clearly.

Device permissions

The current version requests no device permissions: it does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications. If a future feature needed one, it will be requested only at that moment and you will be able to accept it, refuse it or change it in iOS settings.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If any privacy-related issue is detected, it can be reported through the contact email.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app adds new features, external services or relevant changes in how it works. The version published on this page is the current reference.
