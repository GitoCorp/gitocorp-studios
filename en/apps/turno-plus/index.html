A scoreboard for every game.

Turno+ was born as a companion to Noche de Juegos: a flexible counter that adapts to the specific game instead of forcing it into a generic scoreboard.

Creation wizard

Answer a few simple questions about how your game is scored and Turno+ builds the right scoreboard: cumulative rounds, a quantity that counts down to zero, lives, group carry-over and more.

Turn wheel

Decide who starts with an animated wheel, save groups of regular players and choose between colour themes.

Virtual dice

4, 6, 12 or 20-sided dice with a real rolling animation, so you can play without the physical dice.

Turno+ privacy

Scoreboards, player groups and games are stored so you can resume them and look up your history. Some features may query BoardGameGeek to fill in public information about a game when you create a new counter.
