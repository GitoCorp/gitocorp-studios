Getting started

A quick guide to NickClick. The same tutorial appears in the app the first time you open it and from the “How it works” menu.

1. Create a collection

To take it seriously or just to keep your figures tidy. Give it a name and an image and choose whether it is open, to keep adding what you have, or a list, if it is a closed series (for example, 12 figures).

2. Add your figures

Tap “+”, take a photo or pick one from the gallery, type its name and, if you like, its traits (hair, clothes, accessories).

3. Remove the background

On its page, tap “Remove background” and choose a colour from the grid. “Restore background” brings back the original photo.

4. Note what you're missing

In collections with a list, “I'm missing” keeps by hand the ones you don't have yet and the bar shows your progress. When you get one, “I've got it!” moves it into your collection with its name.

5. Mark your favourites

Tap the star on the page or when editing to mark a favourite, and filter them later with the filter button.

6. Search, filter and recognise

Use the search bar, the filter button (order, favourites, colour and family) or the “Who is it?” tab to identify a figure from a photo.

7. On iPhone and iPad

With the same iCloud account, your collections appear on all your devices. From the menu you can add more users; each one has their own collections.

NickClick privacy

Collections, photos and names are stored on your device and synced with your private iCloud between iPhone and iPad. NickClick has no accounts, ads or analytics, and background removal and photo recognition happen on the device.
