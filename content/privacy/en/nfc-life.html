NFC Life is an app by GitoCorp Studios. The public brand is GitoCorp Studios and the responsible developer is Jorge Sanz Borrell. For privacy or support enquiries, you can write to gito@gitocorp.com.

Data the app processes

The app uses the information needed to inventory NFC tags and the objects you link them to: object name, notes, category, date of last check and maintenance reminders.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app's information for personalised advertising.
  • We do not request sensitive data for normal use of the app.
  • The data entered by the user is used to show their inventory and reminders inside the app itself.

NFC reading and writing

The app accesses the iPhone's NFC reader only when the user starts reading or writing a tag (compatible with NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216). iOS will ask for the corresponding permission before using this feature and it can be revoked in system settings.

Storage

The inventory of tags and objects is stored on the device. If the app turns on iCloud sync, the data is also stored in the user's own iCloud account to keep it available across their devices; GitoCorp Studios has no access to that data.

Family sharing

If the user turns on “Family sharing”, the items they choose to share (including their photos, invoices and maintenance records) are stored in the user's own iCloud through CloudKit and shared with the people they invite, who access it with their own iCloud account. Each item is shared only if the user marks it. GitoCorp Studios does not receive or have access to that data, and the user can stop sharing at any time from the app.

Device permissions

In addition to NFC access, if notifications are turned on for maintenance reminders, iOS will ask for authorisation beforehand and they can be turned off in system settings.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If any privacy-related issue is detected, it can be reported through the contact email.
