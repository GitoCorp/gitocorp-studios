Data the app processes

Disfrazados is a deduction game designed for children too, and it stores only what is essential to play. Specifically, it stores on your device:

  • The detectives' names you type when setting up a game, to suggest them next time. They can be nicknames; there is no need to use real names.
  • Game settings: difficulty, number of guests, mode, surprises, power cards and vibration.
  • The album: the culprits caught, with the date and the name of the detective who caught them, and the VIP guests discovered.
  • Daily Challenge results, to respect one attempt per day and to calculate the streak.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • The app has no user accounts.
  • It contains no advertising, in-app purchases, chat or analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not request sensitive data.

Storage

All of Disfrazados's information is stored only on the device where you play (iPhone, iPad or Apple TV). The app does not use iCloud, does not need an internet connection and does not send data to any server, whether GitoCorp Studios's or a third party's. The Daily Challenge is generated from the device's own date, without querying any service. If you delete the app, all its data is deleted with it.

If a future version adds sync, Game Center, leaderboards or other online features, this policy will be updated beforehand to explain it clearly.

Device permissions

The app requests no permissions: it does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications.

Children

Disfrazados is designed to be played by children aged 6 and up together with their family. For that reason it has no advertising, purchases, accounts or communication with other people, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you notice any privacy-related issue, write to us at the contact email.
