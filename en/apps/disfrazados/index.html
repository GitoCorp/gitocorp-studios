Real deduction, sized for little ones.

Disfrazados takes the classic idea of guessing with questions and turns it into a different party every time: guests generated on the spot, surprises that change the board and a rival, Mask, whose difficulty is under your control.

Every game is new

Guests are created for each game by combining colours, bodies, eyes, horns, hats and objects, with traits well spread so every question counts.

Mask, a rival at your level

On Easy, Normal or Expert, Mask plays at a steady pace with no luck-based tricks, so a child can beat it and an adult gets a real challenge.

Modes for the whole family

A duel passing the device, classic mode choosing your costumed guest, “Your turn to answer”, “It's getting away!” and a Daily Challenge with a single try.

Disfrazados privacy

Disfrazados stores everything on the device only: detectives' names, settings, the album of culprits, VIP guests and Daily Challenge results. It has no accounts, ads, purchases or analytics, needs no internet and asks for no permissions.
