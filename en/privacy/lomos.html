Lomos privacy policy

Lomos privacy.

What Lomos stores about your library, how it syncs and is shared with your family, what it looks up online and which permissions it uses.

Controller

Lomos is an app by GitoCorp Studios. The public brand is GitoCorp Studios and the responsible developer is Jorge Sanz Borrell. For privacy or support enquiries, you can write to gito@gitocorp.com.

Data the app processes

Lomos stores the information you enter to organise your home library:

  • Books: title, author, publisher, year, pages, ISBN, synopsis, category, cover, spine colour, notes, favourites and the shelf where they are.
  • Family readers: a name, a colour and a group. No email, phone number, date of birth or any other personal data is requested.
  • Reading: the page each reader is on, their list of upcoming reads (books at home), their yearly reading goal, how many times each book has been read and ratings with their comments.
  • Loans to friends or family: the name of the person who borrows the book and, if you enter them, a contact detail and the return date.
  • Books from a public library: the name of the library and the date they must be returned.
  • Wishlist: the books you would like to have or read, who they are for, whether they have been read and, if you add them, shops, web links and prices.
  • Home and bookcases: rooms, walls, furniture with its measurements and shelves and, if you choose that option, a photo of a bookcase.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app's information for advertising or analytics.
  • We do not request sensitive data for normal use of the app.

Storage and iCloud sync

Data is stored on your device and, if iCloud is turned on, synced through CloudKit in the private database of your own iCloud account, so your library is available on your iPhone and iPad. That information is only accessible with your Apple account: GitoCorp Studios has no access to it and does not receive it on any server of its own. Syncing is governed by Apple's iCloud terms.

Lomos has no user accounts of its own. If you are not signed in to iCloud, the data stays on the device only. To delete synced data, delete the library from the app or delete Lomos's data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Sharing the library with your family

If you invite someone from Settings › Family, that person will be able to see and edit the whole shared library (books, readers, reading, reading lists and goals, ratings, loans, wishlist and bookcases) with their own iCloud account, through iCloud's sharing system. Whoever shares can remove access at any time, and whoever receives the invitation can leave from Settings. Nobody else can see a library you have not shared with them.

Looking up book details

To fill in a book's details and cover, Lomos sends the scanned ISBN or the title and author you search for to Google Books (Google), Open Library (Internet Archive), the public Apple Books catalogue (Apple) and the catalogue of the National Library of Spain, and downloads the cover from the first three. Nothing else is sent: not your name, your library or any family data. As with any online request, those services receive your connection's IP address and handle the request under their own privacy policies.

If you add your own Google Books key in Settings, it is stored only on your device and sent only to Google along with your searches.

Device permissions

  • Camera: only when you use it to read a book's barcode or take a photo of a cover or a bookcase. The barcode is read on the device. If you photograph a cover to look a book up, the title and author are read on the device and only that text is sent to the search; the photo never leaves your iPhone.
  • Photos: the app uses the system photo picker, which only gives it access to the images you choose; it does not access the rest of your library.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, Lomos reminds you 2 days before and on the day a book must be returned to a public library and, if you leave it on, encourages you to read when 3 weeks go by without you logging any reading. These reminders are scheduled on the device itself, without going through any server, and each one can be turned off in the app's Settings.

Lomos does not request access to your location, microphone, contacts or calendar.

Children

Lomos is designed to be used as a family. Readers without a device, such as children, are just a name and a colour inside your library and are managed by the adults. The app does not collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Terms of use

  • Lomos is provided as is, for personal, non-commercial use.
  • Book details come from Google Books, Open Library, Apple Books and the National Library of Spain and may be incomplete or contain errors; you can correct them by hand.
  • The furniture drawings are approximate and do not reproduce exact commercial models. Brand names shown in the app belong to their respective owners.
  • Return reminders are an aid: the date that counts is the one set by your library.
  • The app may change or stop offering features over time. Relevant changes will be reflected on this page.

Changes to these texts

These texts may be updated when the app adds new features, external services or relevant changes in how it works. The version published on this page is the one in force.
