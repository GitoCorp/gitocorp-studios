What FamilyBank is

FamilyBank is a family bank with pretend money: it lets a family keep balances, allowances, savings, piggy banks and points. The app makes no real payments, does not connect to bank accounts and does not ask for cards, account numbers or any other real financial data.

Data the app processes

To work, the app stores the information the family itself enters: members' names, their role (director or client), balances, transactions, descriptions, piggy banks, savings plans, points, rewards and redemptions, as well as the identifier of the NFC card associated with each member.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app's information for advertising or for tracking across apps.
  • Each family has its own data space, separate from those of other families.
  • Photos chosen to personalise a card are stored only on the device and are not uploaded to any server.

Accounts, children and adult control

The app is designed for family use. Children's accounts are created and managed by the director adults of the family, who can see every member's transactions, pay the allowance, add or deduct points and reset the family's data. FamilyBank is not aimed at children using it on their own outside that family setting.

Ways to sign in

Adults can sign in with Apple. Within the app, access to each account can be done with the NFC card (which only identifies the member), with Face ID or with Touch ID. Face or fingerprint recognition is performed by iOS itself: the app does not receive or store biometric data. Director access is additionally protected with a password.

Third-party services

The app uses Firebase, from Google, for authentication, storage and syncing of the family's data between their devices, and to run server functions that send notifications. Notifications are delivered through Apple's push notification service. These services process the data needed to provide their function under their own terms and policies; GitoCorp Studios does not control their servers.

Device permissions

  • NFC: used only when the user holds a card up to identify themselves.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: handled by iOS; the app only receives whether authentication succeeded.
  • Notifications: iOS asks for authorisation before sending them and they can be turned off in system settings. They are used to notify of deposits, transactions and requests within the family.
  • Photo library: only if the user decides to use a photo as the background of their card.

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept while the family uses the app. Directors can reset the family's finances and remove members from the director panel. To request deletion of a family's data, write to gito@gitocorp.com.
