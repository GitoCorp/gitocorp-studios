Data the app processes

The app uses the information needed to offer its game modes. It may store local progress, preferences, scores, game statistics, selected categories or data needed to keep the pace of a session.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app's information for personalised advertising.
  • We do not request sensitive data for normal use of the app.
  • Scores and progress are used to show results inside the app and to improve the game experience.

Storage and Apple services

Information is stored on the device whenever the feature allows it. If a version uses Game Center, iCloud, achievements, leaderboards or games with other users, the information associated with those services will be handled through Apple and will depend on the user's settings.

Device permissions

The app will only request permissions when they are needed for a specific feature. The user can accept, refuse or change those permissions in iOS settings.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If any privacy-related issue is detected, it can be reported through the contact email.
