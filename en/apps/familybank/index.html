A bank at family scale.

FamilyBank recreates the experience of a serious banking app, but with pretend money and under the control of the adults at home.

Director and client roles

Director adults pay the allowance, award points and see every transaction. Clients withdraw, transfer and save within their limits.

Saving with a purpose

An untouchable individual savings fund and piggy banks for specific goals, with clear contributions and colours by type of movement.

Points and rewards

Each family builds its own rewards catalogue, and clients can ask for points for a job well done and redeem them for their rewards.

FamilyBank privacy

FamilyBank does not handle real money or ask for bank details. Each family's data is stored in its own separate space, and the photos used to personalise a card stay on the device only.
