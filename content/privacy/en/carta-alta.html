Data the app processes

Carta Alta is a card game for two people on the same device. It stores on your device only what is needed to play:

  • Player profiles: the name or nickname you type and a colour. There is no need to use real names.
  • Each profile's progress: victory points and coins earned, powers obtained in the shop, the last power deck chosen and the number of games played and won. Points and the shop are pretend: there is no real money and there are no purchases.
  • Settings: deck theme, chosen card back, unlocked card backs, number of rounds and type of deal.
  • The photo decks you create: their name, the names of the figures and the photos you choose for them.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • The app has no user accounts.
  • It contains no advertising, in-app purchases, chat or analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not request sensitive data.

Photos in custom decks

If you create a deck with photos, you choose each photo one by one with the system photo picker. The app only receives the photos you choose; it has no access to the rest of your photo library and does not ask your permission to see it.

Automatic face framing and the option to remove the background are done on the device itself, with Apple's tools. The app stores the original photo you choose and the cut-out version so that you can adjust it again. No photo is sent to the internet, to GitoCorp Studios or to third parties.

If you use photos of other people, make sure they agree, especially if they are minors. You can remove a photo or delete a whole deck at any time from the app itself; when you do, its photos are deleted from the device.

Storage

All of Carta Alta's information is stored only on the device where you play. The app does not use iCloud, does not need an internet connection and does not send data to any server. If you delete the app, all its data and photos are deleted with it.

If a future version adds sync, Game Center, online games or other network features, this policy will be updated beforehand to explain it clearly.

Device permissions

The app requests no permissions: it does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or notifications, and photos are chosen with the system picker without giving access to the whole photo library.

Children

Carta Alta is suitable for the whole family. It has no advertising, purchases, accounts or communication with other people, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you notice any privacy-related issue, write to us at the contact email.
