Turno+ privacy policy

Turno+ privacy.

How scoreboards, player groups and game searches are handled, and the terms of use.

Controller

Turno+ is an app by GitoCorp Studios. The public brand is GitoCorp Studios and the responsible developer is Jorge Sanz Borrell. For privacy or support enquiries, you can write to gito@gitocorp.com.

Data the app processes

The app uses the information needed to create and use scoreboards: player names, colours, results, game history and saved player groups for the turn wheel.

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the app's information for personalised advertising.
  • We do not request sensitive data for normal use of the app.
  • The data entered by the user is used to show their scoreboards, groups and history inside the app itself.

BoardGameGeek and intermediary server

When creating a counter for a specific game, the app may search BoardGameGeek to obtain public information such as the title, cover or year. That search goes through an intermediary server of GitoCorp Studios (hosted on Supabase) that forwards to BoardGameGeek only the text you type in the search box. No player name, group, score or personal identifier is sent, and no profile of you is kept. GitoCorp Studios does not control BoardGameGeek; its own terms and privacy policy apply to that query.

Storage

Scoreboards, favourites, player groups, colours, names and game history are stored only on your device. Turno+ has no user accounts, includes no analytics or advertising and does not upload that data to any server. The Apple Watch app works independently and also stores its scoreboards only on the watch, with no internet connection. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. If a future version adds sync or a connection with another GitoCorp Studios app, this policy will be updated beforehand to explain it clearly.

Device permissions

Turno+ does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos, and does not send notifications.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If any privacy-related issue is detected, it can be reported through the contact email.

Terms of use

Turno+ is a support tool for keeping scores, turns and dice in board and card games. By using it you accept the following:

  • It is provided as is, for personal, non-commercial use. Although care is taken for the sums and totals to be correct, check important results yourself; GitoCorp Studios is not liable for losses arising from a scoring error.
  • The randomness of the wheel and the dice is generated by the device and must not be used for gambling with money.
  • The names, covers and information of third-party games belong to their respective owners. BoardGameGeek information is shown courtesy of that service, which is not affiliated with Turno+.
  • You are responsible for the names and data you type into the app and for respecting the privacy of the people you play with.
  • The app may change or stop offering features over time. Relevant changes will be reflected on this page.

Changes to these texts

These texts may be updated when the app adds new features, external services or relevant changes in how it works. The version published on this page is the current reference.

Contact

For questions about privacy, support or deletion of information, write to gito@gitocorp.com.
